📘 Learn About Carbon Footprint

Simple and easy information for everyone

What is it?

A carbon footprint is the total greenhouse gas (mostly CO₂) released because of the things we do every day. It is counted in kg or tonnes of CO₂ per year.

Why does it matter?

Too much CO₂ traps heat in the atmosphere. This is called the greenhouse effect and it causes global warming, melting glaciers, floods, heat waves and rising sea levels.

🌫️ Main greenhouse gases

GasMain source
Carbon dioxide (CO₂)Burning coal, petrol, diesel and gas
Methane (CH₄)Farming, cattle, landfills
Nitrous oxide (N₂O)Fertilisers and agriculture
1️⃣

Energy

Power plants burning coal and gas are the biggest source worldwide.

2️⃣

Transport

Cars, buses, trucks, ships and planes burn fuel.

3️⃣

Industry

Making cement, steel and plastic needs a lot of energy.

4️⃣

Food & Farming

Land use, cattle and fertiliser release CO₂, methane and N₂O.

✅ 10 easy ways to reduce your footprint

  • Switch off lights, fans and chargers when not in use.
  • Use LED bulbs and energy-efficient (star rated) appliances.
  • Walk, cycle, or use bus / metro / carpool instead of a car.
  • Use a train instead of a plane for short trips.
  • Eat more plant-based food and do not waste food.
  • Reduce single-use plastic; carry a cloth bag and water bottle.
  • Reuse, repair and recycle things before buying new ones.
  • Save water; fix leaking taps.
  • Plant trees and take care of them.
  • Use solar energy where possible and tell others about it.

🇮🇳 India and the world

India has set a goal to reach net zero emissions by 2070. Everyone can help by making small changes. The "1.5°C goal" in our chart is the yearly per-person level scientists suggest to keep global warming within safe limits (about 2.3 tonnes by 2030).
