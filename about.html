Who we are and why we built this website

🎯 Our Mission

To make people aware of their carbon footprint in a simple way, and to motivate them to take small steps that protect our planet.

👁️ Our Vision

A cleaner, greener future where every person knows their impact on the environment and works to reduce it.

About the Project

The Carbon Footprint Awareness Platform is a Web Technology project. It lets a registered user calculate yearly CO₂ emissions from electricity, travel, LPG and diet, view charts, compare with the India and world average, get personalised eco tips and download a PDF report.

⭐ Features

  • Secure login and sign-up with email format and 10-digit phone validation
  • Carbon footprint calculator with Eco Score, carbon level and trees needed
  • Doughnut and comparison bar charts (Chart.js)
  • Personalised eco suggestions based on your biggest emission source
  • Emission history saved separately for each user
  • Downloadable PDF report (jsPDF)
  • Learn page with simple information and a quiz
  • Responsive design for mobile, tablet and desktop

🛠️ Technologies Used

HTML5 · CSS3 (Flexbox, Grid, animations) · JavaScript (DOM, Regular Expressions, LocalStorage) · Chart.js · jsPDF
